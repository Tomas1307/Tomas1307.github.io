MARSA: A Multi-accent, Multi-system Corpus of Full and Partial Voice Spoofing Attacks for Spanish Anti-spoofing Research in preparation
T. Acosta-Bernal, J.C. Vásquez-Correa, M.D. Calderón-Acosta, R.F. Manrique · manuscript for Scientific Data (Nature)
dataset · doi:10.5281/zenodo.23873176code

Voice cloning is now good enough to fool people and speaker-verification systems, and almost all anti-spoofing data is in English. MARSA is the first large-scale Spanish corpus for the problem: 240,995 utterances built from 35,927 genuine recordings of 1,567 speakers across 7 accent regions, attacked with 6 open voice-cloning systems.

Besides fully synthetic utterances it includes word-level partial spoofs, where only some words are replaced, which are much harder to detect. It is released in the ASVspoof 2019 Logical Access format with frame-level labels, so existing detectors can be trained and compared on it directly. This is the basis of my master's thesis.

Eyes in the Sky: Deep Learning Processing of Airborne Sensor Imagery for Environmental Monitoring in the Colombian Amazon published
T. Acosta-Bernal, S. Romero, J.A. Carasquilla, J.P. Reyes, H.M. Núñez, M. Linares-Vásquez, A. Zabala-López · 2025 IEEE International Workshop on Technologies for Defense and Security (TechDefense), Rome
doi:10.1109/TECHDEFENSE67306.2025.11721025code

Illegal mining camps in the Amazon are hard to find from the ground. We trained YOLO detectors on aerial thermal (FLIR) video, comparing preprocessing strategies and hyperparameter configurations to favor recall, since a missed camp costs more than a false alarm. The best configuration reached 93.4% mAP@50.

Multi-Agent Evolution for Electric Vehicle Charging Systems: A Scatter Search Approach to Breeding Specialized DQN Agents in progress
T. Acosta Bernal, A. Tabares
code

Scheduling many electric vehicles on a constrained charging station is a hard optimization problem, and exact MILP models stop finding solutions as it grows. We use scatter search to evolve the hyperparameters of Deep Q-Network agents into five specialized archetypes and combine them with MILP. Across 592 vehicles, the hybrid solves instances where MILP alone was infeasible on 35% of the test systems.
